Processing

These stages need no model download. Dust removal targets tiny isolated marks; deblur reduces mild defocus. Gentle denoise preserves more texture than strong AI denoise.

New photos start with these stages selected. Retouch still runs one photo at a time, and only when you ask for that photo. Opening a photo or scan also applies these defaults to photos that have not been retouched yet. Retouched photos keep their own stages.

Balance faded color prints and restore washed-out black/white levels. These stages run before colorization and need no model download.

AI enlargement runs a 4× network, then resizes to the selected output size. Strong AI denoise uses its denoising network once, including at original output size.

Invents detail that was not on the print. The preview is marked as a reconstruction.

Weights stay out of the project. A download runs only after you accept that model's license, including when a current file is downloaded again. Each file is an ONNX conversion of the official weights, checked against a pinned checksum.
